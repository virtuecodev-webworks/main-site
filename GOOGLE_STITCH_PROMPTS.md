## 1. Pro-Tip: Upload Your Brand Image Directly Into Google Stitch!

Google Stitch ([stitch.withgoogle.com](https://stitch.withgoogle.com/)) is **multi-modal**—it accepts **an image attachment + a text prompt** at the same time.
1. Open **Google Stitch** and **attach/upload your Brand Sheet image** (`Virtue&Co WEBWORKS` brand board).
2. Paste **Prompt 1** below.
3. Because Stitch sees your exact `#D4AF37` Gold / `#0B0B0D` Charcoal Black / `#FFFFFF` White brand sheet AND reads the architectural prompt, it will preserve your exact logo lockup while generating an **Awwwards-level, non-generic layout**.
